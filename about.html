About This Directory

Built for the amputee community.

Losing a limb changes how you move through the world — but it doesn't have to mean facing it alone. There are dozens of support groups, exercise classes, podcasts, and nonprofits built specifically for amputees and their families. The problem was never that these resources didn't exist. It's that they were scattered across Facebook groups, word-of-mouth, and hard-to-find websites.

This directory brings them together in one place: online Zoom support groups, adaptive exercise classes, podcasts and media, nonprofits offering financial assistance, and communities where amputees connect with people who understand.

It's free to use, and it always will be. We're not a medical provider and we don't diagnose or treat anything — we're a signpost pointing you toward people and organizations who can help.

This list grows with help from the community. If you know a resource that belongs here, please submit it.
